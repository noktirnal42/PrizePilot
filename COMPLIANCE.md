# PrizePilot Compliance Policy

PrizePilot is a preparation and review tool. It never submits entries, accepts terms, creates accounts, pays fees, uploads tax documents, invents claims, or bypasses access controls.

## Blocked Actions

Blocked actions show:

> This action requires explicit human approval and must be completed manually outside PrizePilot.

Blocked categories include automatic submission, terms acceptance, payment or tax steps, fake identities, fake credentials, fake nonprofit claims, access-control bypass, private-data exposure, and unauthorized security testing.

## Organizational Claims

The user may have an LLC called First Nomadic Church of Bass, LLC. PrizePilot treats it only as an inactive or early-stage LLC unless the user provides documentation. The app must not claim nonprofit, church, 501(c)(3), grant-eligible community-program, endorsement, or public-benefit status without verified proof.

## Human Approval Policy

PrizePilot may draft, summarize, score, and plan. The user must manually verify official rules, eligibility, IP terms, tax notes, account requirements, and final submission materials. Any final submission, terms acceptance, fee payment, account creation, or tax upload happens outside PrizePilot.

## Security Work

Security bounty opportunities are allowed only after official scope and safe harbor are clear. Destructive tests, out-of-scope testing, scanning without permission, exploitation, and private-data access are blocked.
